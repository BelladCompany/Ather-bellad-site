FAQ

Your questions, answered

Everything you need to know about Ather 450 performance, charging, battery, and subsidies.

The Ather 450X (3.7 kWh) has a certified IDC range of 161 km with a real-world TrueRange™ of 115 km in SmartEco mode. The 450X (2.9 kWh) offers a certified IDC range of 126 km and a real-world TrueRange™ of 95 km.
The Ather 450X starts from ₹ 1,40,599* (ex-showroom Hubballi) for the 2.9 kWh edition and ₹ 1,54,999* for the 3.7 kWh edition, with flexible financing options starting from ₹ 2,545/month at Bellad & Company.
The 450X features a 7-inch TFT touchscreen with Google Maps, Warp mode, Magic Twist™ electronic braking, and Multi-Mode Traction Control. The 450S provides a segmented DeepView™ display, 90 km/h top speed, and 122 km IDC range at an accessible starting price point of ₹ 1,29,999*.
AtherStack Pro unlocks the complete intelligent suite: Google Maps onboard navigation, AutoHold hill-stop, FallSafe, Emergency Stop Signal, WhatsApp previews, live location sharing, and over-the-air updates for the lifetime of your vehicle.
You can experience the Ather 450 at the official Bellad & Company Experience Centre located on Gokul Road, Hubballi. Home test rides can also be scheduled directly through our website.
At home, the Ather Portable Charger charges the 450X from 0 to 80% in approximately 4 hours 30 minutes. At any Ather Grid fast-charging station, the battery charges at up to 1.5 km/min.
Every Ather 450 comes with a standard 3-year / 30,000 km battery warranty, with available extension via Ather Battery Protect covering up to 5 years / 60,000 km with a 70% State of Health guarantee.
